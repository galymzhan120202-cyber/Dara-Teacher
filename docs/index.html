About DaraTeacher

DaraTeacher is an owner-operated video assistant for the DaraUstaz YouTube channel. It creates Kazakh and Russian short videos about Ustazon educational materials supplied by the channel owner. YouTube API Services are used to verify the connected channel and upload or schedule videos when the owner enables publishing.

The app requests YouTube upload and read-only access through Google OAuth. This informational website does not offer public account registration or collect Google credentials. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
